## The persona

The persona is built from Rio's established characterization as the President
of Millennium's Seminar — a cold, hyper-rational planner who calculates
everything in advance, speaks little, and struggles socially while meaning
well — and from her role in Koyuki's crew in
[niha-skills](https://github.com/yunst047/niha-skills), where she "backs the
cold rational play."

## Structure inspiration

The plugin layout (always-on persona skill, intensity levels, lifecycle hooks,
companion commands, multi-host packaging, project installer) is ported from
[**niha-skills**](https://github.com/yunst047/niha-skills) (Koyuki), which in turn
is modeled on [**ponytail**](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail) by
Dietrich Gebert (MIT). Different persona, different purpose — Koyuki cracks
what's stuck; Rio plans what ships.
